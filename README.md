## About

Most of my work is mobile, built in React Native, though I'm comfortable across the stack with Next.js on the front end and Node.js or NestJS on the back end. I've spent a lot of time on apps that have to work in more than one language and region, and on the backend and automation that sits behind them.

A couple of recent things worth looking at:

**[HelloRoam](https://github.com/HiZouraiz/helloroam-case-study)** is a global eSIM travel app, live on iOS and Android in more than 190 countries and 22 languages, with a 5 out of 5 rating on the App Store. I worked on it as a senior team lead, running both the mobile app team and the website team. I also built the AI engine behind its content, doing SEO keyword research, translation into 22 languages, and localized image generation from a single source.

**[Nova Auto Spa](https://github.com/HiZouraiz/nova-auto-spa-case-study)** is a car wash marketplace I co-founded at AzCodeWorks. It has two apps, one for customers and one for the detailers, and runs across the US and Mexico in English and Spanish.

If you're hiring for React Native or full-stack work, the case studies above have more detail. You can reach me at officialzouraiz@gmail.com.
